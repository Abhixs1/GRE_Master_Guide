## Questions 101-110

**101.** Such ______ behavior, flouting every convention, scandalized the conservative town.

> `A` circumspect\
> `B` seemly\
> `C` demure\
> `D` brazen\
> `E` decorous

**102.** The drug offered a ______ cure, eliminating the disease in a single dose.

> `A` modest\
> `B` limited\
> `C` negligible\
> `D` partial\
> `E` remarkable

**103.** So ______ was the demand for tickets that the show sold out in minutes.

> `A` intense\
> `B` slight\
> `C` feeble\
> `D` tepid\
> `E` modest

> `A` tenacity\
> `B` yieldingness\
> `C` pliability\
> `D` compliance\

> `A` ruthless\

> `A` token\
> `B` halfhearted\
> `C` feeble\
> `D` obdurate\
> `E` negligible

**107.** His ______ in crisis, calm while others panicked, marked him for command.

> `A` hysteria\
> `B` agitation\

> `D` panic\
> `E` frenzy

> `A` disjointed\
